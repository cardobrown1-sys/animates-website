Profiles that give you something to talk about

Everyone on Animates answers three prompts, so your first message can be about more than "hey". Here are a few prompts with example answers.

  • My comfort anime is…

    Anything with a found family and a cooking scene.

  • We'll get along if you can handle my take on…

    The manga ending. I have slides.

  • My dream cosplay is…

    The one I keep promising to finish before the next con.

  • A perfect weekend for me looks like…

    New episode, good ramen, phone on silent.

What you'll find inside

  • Communities and watch parties

    Join fandom hubs with group chat rooms, and plan watch parties with people who are following the same shows.

  • Notice Me

    Send one standout like with a short message, so the person knows exactly why you picked them.

  • You choose what shows

    Pronouns, sexuality, height and lifestyle answers each have their own switch. If a switch is off, nobody else sees that answer.

  • Your location stays vague

    Other people only see a rounded distance, such as "5 mi away". They never see your address or a pin on a map.

  • Verified profiles

    Record a short selfie video to earn a verified badge. We delete the video once it has been checked.

  • Block and report anywhere

    When you block someone, you disappear from each other across the whole app. Reports go to our team with the evidence attached.
